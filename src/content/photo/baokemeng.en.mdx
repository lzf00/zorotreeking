The set follows the route of a walk: shop windows on the street first, then the collaboration displays inside,
then the merchandise on the shelves. The same Pokémon appears at different scales and in different materials —
a three-dimensional window display, artwork on packaging, rows of figures — so reading the frames in sequence shows
how the displays differ far better than any single shot. Open any photo to view it larger; they stay in the order
they were taken. Characters and artwork belong to their rights holders, and this is a personal record.
