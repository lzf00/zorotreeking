Six frames in display order: the full arrangement at the entrance first, then each character up close — Zoro, Ace
and the rest — and finally the scene details. The subject is the layering of the display: the figure in front, the
backdrop behind it, and how the lighting lands on different materials. It is a short set, but each frame works on
its own as a still life. Characters and artwork belong to their rights holders.
