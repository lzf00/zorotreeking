Five frames built around one palette: purple and the butterfly motif run through the set, with the figure in front and
the darker part of the scene holding it up behind. These are still lifes, but the point is colour — which shade of
this character reads most strongly — so each frame lets one area stand out and quiets the rest. Characters and
artwork belong to their rights holders.
