# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32351" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LookAnything: A Foundation Model for Holistic Gaze Following</a>

*HF ★ 0 · Jiayu Tang, Yuchen Zhou, Hongsheng Wu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32351)*

Existing gaze following methods rely on auxiliary inputs such as head pose and depth, have poor generalization, and only support single output formats. To address these issues, this paper proposes LookAnything, a general foundation model. Its core innovation is the introduction of learnable gaze prior queries, which eliminate the need for auxiliary inputs. Trained across domains on the self-built multi-task benchmark HoliGaze, it supports multiple output formats and various gaze-related tasks. Experiments show that its performance surpasses 33 mainstream vision-language models, matches specialized detection models, and delivers excellent cross-domain generalization and real-world deployment performance.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30564" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Seeing Is Locating: Spatial Awareness and Geographic Bias in Decision Language Models</a>

*HF ★ 1 · Wilson Wongso, Hada Melino Muhammad, Xiachong Lin… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30564)*

The spatial awareness capabilities and geographic biases of decision-making large models that output label distributions are not yet well understood. This study constructs GeoDecisions, a multimodal evaluation benchmark that includes two types of tasks: geographically-aware image classification and multi-theme location scoring. Evaluation finds that the model's spatial awareness relies on visual input, text-only coordinate information is insufficient, and there is significant geographic bias, with lower scores for regions with low socioeconomic levels. The study also proposes a training-free optimization scheme that can improve spatial awareness without significantly exacerbating bias.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32393" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Fractal Neural Synthesis: Constant-Storage Procedural Weight Derivation via Complex Quadratic Boundary Dynamics</a>

*HF ★ 1 · Volkan Dagli, Zerrin Dagli, Daghan Dagli · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32393)*

Traditional neural networks have a large number of parameters, and flash memory usage increases linearly with parameters, making it difficult to adapt to edge microcontrollers. To address this pain point, this study proposes a fractal neural synthesis architecture: only a 24-byte seed anchored at the complex quadratic bifurcation boundary is needed to programmatically generate hidden layer weights, with a dedicated sampling design and proven entropy bounds. Actual measurements show that its accuracy is 3.6~10.8 percentage points higher than the INT8 linear baseline, and flash memory usage is up to 129 times lower than Float32 MLP.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25946" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Point Dynamics as Proxy for Robotic World Models</a>

*HF ★ 1 · Dong An, Tianyu Liu, Haoran Ding… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25946)*

Existing robotic world models are prone to distortion of object motion after interaction. This study splits the dynamics prediction and visual rendering links, and introduces 3D point dynamics as an intermediate proxy: convert the first frame to 3D points, simulate the action response through a learned point dynamics model, and then render it into a video. Experiments show that this method can reduce the object motion error of datasets and real dual-arm robot scenarios by more than 6% without reducing image quality, and optimizing dynamics modeling can effectively improve the fidelity of world models.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27404" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Degradation of Language Models under Controlled Load: Synchronization, Rule Execution and Misfires on the Background</a>

*HF ★ 0 · Hennadii Kutomanov · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27404)*

The causes of performance degradation of large models under increased load are unclear. This study divides degradation into three categories: synchronization deviation, rule execution errors, and random mismodifications. It controls five types of variables such as text length and number of rules to carry out replacement tests, combines output alignment and statistical models to fit the load-dependent law of degradation, and generates a "performance passport" for the model. Performance passports for 7 models have been completed, providing a new path for fine-grained behavior research of neural networks.

This paper sorts out the research context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed the concept of superintelligent machines, referring to systems that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better systems on their own to achieve iteration; In 2008, Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop where AI relies on existing intelligence to optimize its own cognitive architecture.
