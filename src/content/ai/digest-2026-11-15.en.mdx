# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article focuses on the theme of engineering AI self-improvement, sorting out the conceptual evolution of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good proposed the concept of ultra-intelligent machines, referring to agents that can surpass all human intellectual activities and autonomously design better systems to complete iterations. In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky officially named the concept RSI, clarifying that its core is a feedback loop where AI optimizes its cognitive architecture relying on its own intelligence. In current practice, this can be manifested as directly rewriting its own weights or optimizing training processes.
