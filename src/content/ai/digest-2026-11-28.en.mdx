# OpenAI

## <a href="https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GPT-6 models demonstrate intrinsic protein-design capabilities</a>

*OpenAI*

Recent research confirms that the GPT-6 large model has inherent protein design capabilities: without special adaptation for the protein field, relying solely on its own inference and code generation capabilities, it can complete the design of protein structures and sequences. Laboratory tests show that its designed products for 5 different targets all have clear binding activity detected, providing new possibilities for general large models to be applied in biological R&D scenarios.

*OpenAI*

Two amateur astronomers used Codex and Astra tools to mine public observation data from NASA's Kepler and TESS space telescopes, and successfully screened out several potential distant exoplanet candidates. This result confirms that lightweight AI tools can greatly lower the threshold for scientific research access, empower civilian researchers to participate in cutting-edge astronomical exploration, and also provide a feasible new path for digging deeper into the value of public aerospace scientific research data and public participation in exoplanet searches.

# Google DeepMind

## <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/interpreting-mass-spectra-with-google-antigravity/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">From spectrum to structure: Identifying molecules with Mass Spec Skills for Google Antigravity</a>

*Google DeepMind*

This paper addresses the pain points of traditional mass spectrometry analysis relying on manual experience and low efficiency, and develops the Mass Spec Skills tool adapted for the Google Antigravity platform, which can assist researchers in automatically parsing tandem mass spectrometry data, opening up the identification link from spectra to corresponding molecular structures, effectively improving the efficiency and accuracy of unknown molecule identification, and providing technical support for high-throughput research in scenarios such as metabolomics and natural product analysis.

## <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Finding faster quantum algorithms with agents and formal verification</a>

*Google DeepMind*

This research focuses on the demand for performance upgrades of quantum algorithms in nuclear fusion and computational chemistry scenarios, and innovatively adopts a technical path of multi-agent team collaborative search combined with formal verification: agents are responsible for exploring a large number of potential algorithm structures, and formal verification checks the correctness of algorithm logic, which can efficiently screen out feasible quantum algorithms with better operation speed, providing support for the implementation of quantum computing in related scenarios.

# Hugging Face Blog

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/video-trainium3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Taking Trainium3 to the Track: Generating Videos with LTX-2.5</a>

*Hugging Face*

This paper focuses on the pain point of high computing power cost of AI video large models, and verifies the feasibility of adapting AWS Trainium3 dedicated AI chips to the LTX-2.5 video generation model. Through transformations such as custom operator optimization, distributed parallel strategy adaptation, and dynamic video memory scheduling, compared with mainstream GPUs of the same level, training throughput is increased by more than 30%, and inference generation cost is reduced by 40%, providing a new cost-effective computing power path for large-size long video generation.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llamacpp-amd-bench" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Introducing the llama.cpp benchmark dashboard for AMD Ryzen and Radeon</a>

*Hugging Face*

This launch of the llama.cpp benchmark visualization dashboard for AMD Ryzen CPUs and Radeon graphics cards fills the previous gap where llama.cpp lacked a unified tool for summarizing end-side inference performance on the AMD platform. It can automatically aggregate measured data such as large model inference speed and video memory/memory usage under different hardware configurations and model quantization precision, which not only facilitates users to compare the actual performance of running large models on AMD platforms, but also provides data support for the optimization and iteration of llama.cpp on the AMD side.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the conceptual context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): in 1965, scholar Irving John Good first proposed the relevant idea, referring to superhuman intelligent systems that can design better machines on their own to achieve self-iteration; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI relies on existing intelligence to optimize its own cognitive mechanism. RSI in the current AI context includes both the model directly rewriting its own weights, and can also generally refer to the behavior of optimizing its own training pipeline.
