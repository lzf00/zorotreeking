# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26203" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Focused Omni-Modal GUI Agents</a>

*HF ★ 36 · Fuyao Zhang, Wenwen He, Jiaming Zhang… · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26203)*

To address the problem that full-modal GUI agents are prone to sequence distraction and trajectory drift when processing full context uniformly, this paper proposes FocusGUI, a lightweight adapter: it can dynamically sort the priority of multimodal channels according to single-step decision requirements, compress relevant features and inject them into the frozen decoder, and only relies on action supervision training without fine-tuning the backbone. Experiments show that compared with the frozen backbone, it improves average type matching by 33.2 and exact matching by 3 times, and is 30.1 higher in type matching than existing state-of-the-art enhancement methods.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28578" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Sage-A1.5: From Long-Horizon Execution to Scientific Exploration with Capability Composition</a>

*HF ★ 31 · Wenjie Lou, Tianshuo Peng, Shiyang Feng… · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28578)*

Sage-A1.5 is a 35B parameter MoE architecture agent model developed based on the previous generation A1, with three core optimizations: parallel task orchestration supporting context isolation of multiple sub-agents, a verification mechanism with progress check and policy reflection, and scientific research capabilities for open exploratory research. Compared with the previous generation, it can complete long-cycle complex tasks more reliably and efficiently, and also supports interdisciplinary scientific research exploration, providing a usable foundation for relevant academic research.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28630" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SolarRenderer: Few-Step Generative Reference Rendering</a>

*HF ★ 22 · Guian Fang, Shengju Qian, Dongyue Lu… · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28630)*

Aiming at the pain points of existing video renderers being engine-bound, requiring many sampling steps, and having weak generalization, this paper proposes SolarRenderer, a structure-conditioned renderer, which adapts the frozen large model backbone through two lightweight LoRAs, requiring only 4 sampling steps, with a speed 12.3 times that of 50-step solutions, and its block causal variant can generate 768p videos in real time. Trained on 136,000 aligned clips, it achieves SOTA performance and can generalize to 11 unseen structure representations such as skeletons and hand-drawn sketches.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25844" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SWIFT: Self-guided Weak-to-strong dIFfusion models via Target extrapolation</a>

*HF ★ 18 · Yanjin He, Cuifeng Shen, Wanzhou Lei… · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25844)*

To address the pain points of high training cost of diffusion models and additional overhead of inference guidance, this paper proposes SWIFT, a self-guided weak-to-strong diffusion training method: it reuses the frozen early-stage weak denoiser to extrapolate the regression target, strengthens the underfitting components of the model, and discards the guidance after training with no additional inference overhead. Theoretical proof shows that this method can reduce errors, and in experiments, the ImageNet-256 task outperforms the baseline FID with only half the computing power, and high-quality generation can be achieved without inference guidance.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26067" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PhysSkin++: Real-Time and Generalizable Physics-Based Animation via Self-Supervised Neural Skinning</a>

*HF ★ 12 · Yuanhang Lei, Baofei Liu, Xingxuan Li… · [HF mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26067)*

Aiming at the generalization challenge of real-time physical animation across diverse 3D shapes and discrete representations, this paper proposes the PhysSkin++ physics-aware framework: it uses a neural skinning field autoencoder to generate deformation subspaces without per-object training, paired with a physical self-supervised strategy combined with gradient correction to stabilize constraint optimization, and adds a self-supervised complementary objective to learn elastic responses. Verification shows that it supports real-time controllable animation with collision for multiple types of 3D representations, and has excellent cross-shape generalization performance.

*Hugging Face*

This paper proposes FairStress, a deep evaluation framework for the fairness of LLM decision-making, which breaks through the limitations of traditional surface-level fairness detection by constructing boundary stress test cases covering multiple sensitive attributes and multiple decision scenarios. # Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the conceptual evolution of recursive self-improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed the idea of superintelligent machines, referring to agents that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better systems to complete self-iteration; In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop through which AI upgrades its own cognitive base relying on existing intelligence. The current implementation of RSI in the AI field includes two paths: directly rewriting its own weights and optimizing training pipelines.
