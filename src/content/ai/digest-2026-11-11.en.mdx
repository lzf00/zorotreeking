# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12426" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">dNaViT: Discrete Codes are General-Purpose Visual Representations</a>

*HF ★ 19 · Xiaotong Li, Zhengrong Yue, Xu Huang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12426)*

To explore the upper limit of discrete vocabulary for visual information representation and meet the needs of unified multimodal modeling, this paper proposes dNaViT, a Discrete Native Resolution Vision Transformer: based on a frozen pretraining encoder, it adopts lightweight residual modulation and grouped product quantization design, taking both semantic and pixel information into account. As a general-purpose discrete visual tokenizer, it matches or outperforms dedicated baselines on 17 visual understanding, generation, and editing benchmarks, verifying that compact discrete vocabularies can serve as general visual representations to support unified multimodal paradigms.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12885" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation</a>

*HF ★ 12 · Heng Fang, Zehui Guo, Yuehan Zhang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12885)*

To address the pain points that traditional robot manipulation strategies are bound to training distributions, and existing in-context learning performs poorly due to lack of current environment feedback, the study finds that in-context learning acquires semantic information that can be transferred across scenarios. Based on this, the World In-Context Learning (WICL) paradigm is proposed, and the RoboScript architecture is built. It only requires a single demonstration plus simulation drills to be stored as task memory, no gradient update is needed. Its success rate on 50 manipulation tasks is 20 percentage points higher than the best training strategy, and it can be transferred to real robots across distributions and entities.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14033" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Few-Step Streaming Video Generation via Condition Shifting</a>

*HF ★ 9 · Deyuan Liu, Jungang Li, Kaiwen Zheng… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14033)*

To reduce the training cost of few-step sampling for streaming video generation, this paper proposes a condition shifting mechanism to modify the APEX model. The same set of parameters can realize both content generation and pseudo-sample estimation, eliminating the need for the independent pseudo-score network required by DMD. Experiments show that its instruction following of user preferences is 30% higher than that of S2-Avatar, with better visual effects. Under the same configuration, its VRAM usage is 4.4% lower and iteration speed is 9.2% faster than DMD, only its motion quality is slightly inferior to S2-Avatar.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03142" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DepthGraft: Structural Regularization through Hierarchical Cross-Layer KV Reconstruction</a>

*HF ★ 6 · Xiaohan Qin, Xiangdong Zhang, Yu Wang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03142)*

To address the problems of inter-layer information dilution when scaling up Pre-LN architecture large models, and the high additional overhead of existing cross-layer connection solutions, this paper proposes the DepthGraft hierarchical KV sharing architecture: it reconstructs deep-layer KV using stride-aligned shallow and middle-layer KV pairs, which can serve as a structural regularization term to optimize KV representation, and also reduce KV cache by 1/3. Experiments on 0.5B-16B dense and MoE models show that it generally improves performance on downstream tasks, with a maximum improvement of 7% on MMLU-Pro for the 16B MoE, suitable for large-scale pretraining.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12411" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PhysOPSD: Code as Privilege for Physically Plausible Video Generation</a>

*HF ★ 6 · Harold Haodong Chen, Rui Peng, Lingteng Qiu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.12411)*

To address the weak physical dynamic plausibility of existing video generation models and the inherent flaws of traditional fine-tuning/reinforcement learning optimization, this paper proposes the PhysOPSD method, which introduces executable code as privileged physical context. It converts real physical scenarios into verifiable simulation programs through a dedicated engine, paired with a two-layer privilege mechanism and controllable physical variable design. Experiments show that with only 3,000 samples, its physical plausibility far exceeds the baseline, while retaining general generation quality, and is compatible with multiple types of base models.

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the development context of the Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) concept: in 1965, scholar Irving John Good first proposed that "superintelligent machines" that can surpass all human intellectual activities can design better systems by themselves to achieve iteration; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. Currently, RSI implementation in the AI field can be achieved by directly modifying weights and optimizing training processes.
