# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21003" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">K2-Horizon: A Frontier Fleet of Open Source Language Models</a>

*HF ★ 19 · K2 Team, Zhengzhong Liu, Haonan Li… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21003)*

This paper launches the K2-Horizon series of 6 fully open-source large models, with parameters ranging from 0.9B to 375B, adapted for scenarios including general inference, agent, and scientific research. Among models of the same scale, it ranks top among open-source solutions on code and agent benchmarks. Technically, it proposes optimizations such as mixed-value attention: the sparse model's performance matches that of a 32B dense model trained on the same data, the Uno adapter speeds up decoding by up to 2.2x, and the full end-to-end open-source training process and synthetic data pipeline make it highly reproducible.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17834" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PaperCritic: Training AI Reviewers to Assess the Soundness of Papers</a>

*HF ★ 15 · Hyun Ryu, Ji Yong Cho, Eunho Yang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17834)*

To address the issues of surging submission volumes at academic conferences and existing AI reviewers being prone to interference from subjective human-like evaluations, this paper focuses on the most core and objective dimension of paper scientific validity review. It builds the PaperSound-23K dataset with 23,000 annotated review opinions, uses the F1 score of review precision and recall as the reward, and trains the PaperCritic review model via reinforcement learning. It outperforms state-of-the-art models on ICLR review tasks, and can also be transferred to cross-domain tasks such as reviewing Nature series papers, code/contract audits, etc.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19937" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AllocFlow: End-to-End Portfolio Trading Action Generation from Raw Market Data</a>

*HF ★ 14 · Annan Wang, Yuchen Xie, Yu Cen… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.19937)*

To address the problem of misaligned objectives in traditional asset management pipelines, which separate signal prediction and portfolio construction, this paper proposes the end-to-end flow matching framework AllocFlow: it uses a cross-asset flow Transformer to generate multi-asset joint position weights, and adopts a two-stage training approach of policy supervised imitation + group relative reinforcement learning to optimize efficiency. On the crypto perpetual contract dataset, it achieves an annualized net return of 51.38% and a Calmar ratio of 4.043; on 2026 unseen data without retraining, it reaches a Calmar ratio of 4.90, far outperforming baselines.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07222" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SPILLWAY: Separating Target Admission from Sanitization to Defend Tool-Using Agents against Prompt Injection</a>

*HF ★ 13 · Woosang Lim, Yeseul Chang, Jaewoo Lee · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07222)*

To address the problem that tool-calling agents are vulnerable to prompt injection hijacking, this paper proposes the SPILLWAY defense framework, which separates target admission and sanitization processes: it performs deterministic admission based on structured returns from read-only tools and fixed rules, pre-filters suspicious instructions and retains evidence, and supports manual review. Tests show the success rate of standard injection and forged template attacks is 0, and only 0.7% under reinforcement learning attacks, with almost no additional model overhead. It is universal across models and balances security and usability.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20986" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Calpha: Toward Native Biomolecular Intelligence in a General-Purpose Language Model</a>

*HF ★ 8 · Yong Liu, Zhanpeng Shi, Peicong Lin… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.20986)*

This paper proposes Calpha, a general model that integrates general LLM reasoning and protein structure prediction capabilities. The first version is based on Qwen3.5-9B fused with ESM2 residue features, and can be triggered via text prompts to call the pre-trained SimpleFold head to output single-chain protein structures. Tests show its short-chain prediction performance is comparable to existing baselines, while long-chain performance is slightly inferior. Meanwhile, the model's general language ability (MMLU-Pro subset) drops by about 7 points, and capabilities such as complex prediction will be expanded in the future.

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out research related to recursive self-improvement (RSI): the concept was first proposed by scholar I.J. Good in 1965, who believed that superintelligent systems can surpass humans in all intellectual activities, and can design better machines to achieve self-iteration; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. The study proposes that the RSI path for modern AI includes both directly rewriting its own weights and optimizing the training pipeline.
