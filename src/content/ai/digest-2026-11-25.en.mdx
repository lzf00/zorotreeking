## <a href="https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GPT-6 models demonstrate intrinsic protein-design capabilities</a>

*OpenAI*

This study confirms that the GPT-6 large model has native protein design capabilities. Without targeted custom fine-tuning, it can independently complete protein structure and sequence design work relying solely on its own inference and code generation capabilities. The lab tested the binding activity of the designed proteins against 5 types of targets, all of which returned positive results, verifying the effectiveness of the design and providing a new feasible path for AI-driven biological R&D.

# Hugging Face Blog

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/microduck-obstacle-avoidance" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Microduck Obstacle Avoidance: A Sim2Real Workflow with Isaac Lab and Newton</a>

*Hugging Face*

This study addresses the obstacle avoidance demand of micro bionic duck-shaped aircraft, and proposes a Sim2Real implementation workflow based on Isaac Lab and Newton physics engine: Newton is used to calibrate simulation dynamic parameters to reduce the virtual-reality gap, and the reinforcement learning obstacle avoidance strategy trained on the simulation side is directly migrated to the real aircraft, solving the pain point of high training cost for micro and small aircraft on real machines. The measured obstacle avoidance success rate exceeds 92%, which is about 37% higher than the performance of traditional algorithms.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/robotrack" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RoboTrack: Point tracking for real-world robotic manipulation</a>

*Hugging Face*

This paper proposes RoboTrack, a point tracking framework for robot manipulation in real-world scenarios. Aiming at the pain point that general point trackers are prone to drift and tracking loss due to occlusion, object deformation and dynamic interference, it integrates visual feature matching, robot kinematics priors and temporal consistency check to optimize tracking logic. Its inference speed meets real-time deployment requirements, and its tracking accuracy is 21% higher than the existing SOTA in tasks such as grasping and non-rigid manipulation, significantly improving operational robustness.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the conceptual context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed the definition of superintelligent machines, referring to systems that can surpass all human intelligent activities and design better machines to achieve self-upgrade; In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. Current RSI in the AI field can be divided into two paths: directly rewriting its own weights, and optimizing its own training pipeline.
