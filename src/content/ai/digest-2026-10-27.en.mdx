## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34662" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StreamVibe: Proactive Video Agents for Evolving User Intent</a>

*HF ★ 42 · Chengzhi Liu, Yuxiang Lai, Yuzhe Yang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34662)*

Addressing the limitations of existing vision-language models that struggle to capture implicit, dynamic user intents and cannot adapt to real-time interactions, this research releases the StreamVibe interaction benchmark covering 3 types of user intents with a total of 89,100 samples, and proposes a streaming active interaction solution with a hierarchical memory mechanism. ## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33135" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">WorldCast: World-Consistent Character Video Generation with Large-Angle Camera Control</a>

*HF ★ 37 · Lei Cao, Yu Liu, Wenhua Liao… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33135)*

Targeting the problems of existing character video generation methods that struggle to adapt to large-angle camera movements, and are prone to character geometric distortion, appearance degradation, and identity drift, this paper first constructs CLACT, a large-scale character dataset for large-angle camera trajectories, and then proposes the WorldCast framework: it provides geometric guidance through 3D point clouds, paired with geometrically aligned memory attention to complete perspective information, enabling accurate tracking under large-angle camera movements while stably retaining the geometric, appearance, and identity characteristics of the character.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32285" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Trial by Trace: Benchmarking AI Agent Harnesses for Reliability and Failure Attribution</a>

*HF ★ 23 · Zhiyong Cao, Hengjie Zhu, Yongxin Wang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32285)*

Addressing the gap that existing LLM agent benchmarks mostly focus on models and overall performance, and lack special evaluation of the supporting frameworks for agent operation, this research launches the TraceTrial-Bench benchmark, covering 7 reliability dimensions, 95 expert tasks, and supporting modules such as trajectory tracking and two-layer evaluation. Tests on 4 frameworks show scores ranging from 59.8% to 72.0%, and 31.1% of successfully completed task runs actually have framework defects, providing an auditable evaluation basis for framework optimization.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33169" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">World-in-Context: Interactive Video World Modeling with In-Context Conditioning</a>

*HF ★ 14 · Harold Haodong Chen, Rui Peng, Rongjin Guo… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33169)*

Targeting the problems of existing interactive video world models: vague perspective control, confusion between actions and camera movements, and fragmented control paths, this paper proposes the World-in-Context framework: through an in-context conditioning mechanism, three types of control are unified into explicit tokens and incorporated into the shared self-attention space, supplemented by interactive transmission RoPE to ensure temporal continuity. Its 5B parameter version matches the navigation performance of the 22B baseline, with a perspective switching success rate of 93.8%, far exceeding models of the same scale, and also has data efficiency advantages.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31702" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Collaborative In-Context Learning for Indoor Radio Map Estimation</a>

*Yuanzhe Peng, Jie Xu*

Addressing the pain points of existing supervised methods for indoor radio map estimation that require a large number of annotations and cannot centralize scenario privacy data, this study models it as a collaborative in-context learning problem for the first time, and proposes the Poly-Ripple framework: relying on a large autoregressive vision model, it models the causality of wireless propagation in ripple order, introduces importance-aware aggregation of cross-device knowledge, and requires no fine-tuning. Public benchmark tests show its performance is better than similar baselines, consistent with theoretical analysis.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31716" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Does LeWorldModel Preserve Local Action Geometry?</a>

*Vasil Vasilev, Siddharth Nayak*

This study verifies the ability of the pre-trained LeWorldModel (LeWM) to preserve local action geometry. By matching simulator and model responses, action sensitivity is measured using Gram matrices under two types of control tasks and three perturbation scales. The results show that under small perturbations, the deviation between the model's most sensitive action direction and the real environment is 36°~42°, and the deviation decreases significantly when the perturbation is amplified. LeWM has higher errors at small scales, so action sensitivity needs to be evaluated by scale, and its impact on control remains to be explored.
