# Google DeepMind

## <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/beyond-tree-cover-mapping-the-worlds-forest-types/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Beyond tree cover: mapping the world’s forest types</a>

*Google DeepMind*

*Beyond tree cover: mapping the world's forest types* breaks through the traditional approach of only counting forest coverage, and for the first time achieves fine-grained classified mapping of forest types for each terrestrial plot unit at global scale with 10-meter resolution. The results can support accurate accounting of global forest carbon sinks, biodiversity conservation, dynamic management of forest resources and climate change response research, filling the gap in high-resolution mapping in this field.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

The concept of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) was first proposed by I.J. Good in 1965, referring to the operating logic of superintelligence that can surpass all human intelligent activities and iteratively design better systems; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI relies on existing intelligence to optimize its own cognitive architecture. RSI in the current AI context includes both the path where the model directly rewrites its own weights, and can also broadly refer to the behavior of the model optimizing the training pipeline.

## <a href="https://www.qbitai.com/2026/10/505032.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Enterprise AI needs two world models</a>

*QbitAI*

Currently, enterprise AI is generally stuck in an implementation dilemma: 95% of generative AI pilots have no clear profit and loss returns, and applications mostly stay at the individual employee level, without being converted into actual productivity. Fan Ling, founder of Tezign, proposed a solution at the WTCC forum: enterprises do not need to blindly upgrade general large models, but should build two sets of world models: a subjective model relying on external user data to understand customer preferences, and an enterprise model relying on internal data to adapt to internal process rules.
