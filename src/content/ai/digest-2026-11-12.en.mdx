## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13028" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GTAGym: Real AAA Game Tasks Environment for Evaluation and Reinforcement Learning</a>

*HF ★ 8 · Jiajun Chen, Shicheng Fang, Jiahao Lu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.13028)*

To address the pain point that AAA game agent evaluation relies on manual work and cannot effectively support Reinforcement Learning, this paper proposes GTAGym, an agent evaluation and Reinforcement Learning framework based on GTA V. It comes with 150 built-in task benchmarks covering multiple scenarios, supporting automatic scoring, parallel testing, and batch task generation. Evaluations show that cutting-edge large models achieve a maximum accuracy of 70.9%, while bottlenecks remain for tasks such as air navigation; large models fine-tuned based on this framework see cross-domain task performance improve from 15% to 23%.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10193" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ensemble Routing for Prompt-Adaptive Cost-Aware LLM Inference</a>

*HF ★ 0 · Saul Urso, Emanuele Carlini, Patrizio Dazzi… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.10193)*

To meet the demand of balancing response quality and inference cost for online large model services, this paper proposes the single-model routing method PISA and its ensemble routing extension PISA-E: it estimates the model's adaptation capability via soft clustering combined with model-specific predictors, allowing flexible adjustment of cost-quality preferences during inference without retraining. Experiments show that PISA delivers the optimal cost-performance among all evaluated single-model routing solutions; PISA-E outperforms competing products by up to 2.87 percentage points in in-distribution/out-of-distribution scenarios, and can reduce costs by up to 36%.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/token-pooling" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Token Pooling-Aware Training for Multi-Vector Embedding Models</a>

*Hugging Face*

Aiming at the pain point that valid token information is easily lost in the pooling stage of multi-vector embedding models, this paper proposes a token pooling-aware training paradigm: the screening logic of downstream pooling is embedded in the training stage, guiding the model to actively optimize the discrimination of each token embedding, so that the information retained by pooling better matches the demand of dense retrieval tasks. On public retrieval benchmarks, its accuracy is up to 5% higher than the baseline, with no additional inference overhead.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the conceptual evolution of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, scholar Irving Good first proposed the idea of superintelligent machines, referring to agents that can surpass all human intellectual activities and independently design better systems to achieve iterative upgrades; In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive architecture relying on existing intelligence. In the current AI context, RSI includes both models directly rewriting their own weights, and can broadly cover the behavior of models optimizing their own training pipelines.
