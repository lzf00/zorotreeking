## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06748" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">EvoWorld: Towards Out-of-Sight World Evolution in Generative Video Models</a>

*HF ★ 11 · Yequan Bie, Dengyang Jiang, Zhixuan Chen… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06748)*

To address the pain points of existing generative video models lacking a world state reasoning mechanism and poor causal consistency in outputs, this paper proposes the new research problem of "out-of-viewport causal evolution", and launches the EvoWorld brain-rendering collaboration framework: it uses a multimodal LLM as the "world brain" to maintain states and deduce causal changes, paired with an autoregressive renderer for visualization, and also comes with a dedicated dataset and evaluation benchmark. Experiments verify that while ensuring visual quality, its causal evolution performance is significantly better than existing models.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09894" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Video-Coder: Reinforcing Agentic Coding for Video Generation</a>

*HF ★ 5 · Chaoyang Wang, Sicheng Gao, Junan Hu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.09894)*

To address the pain points of insufficient spatial structure accuracy, motion coherence, and scene controllability in existing video generation, this paper proposes Video-Coder, the first code-driven video generation agent: it can independently write and execute code to build 3D dynamic scenes, provide structural and motion signals for downstream generation, is paired with a dedicated dataset and interactive training environment, and is trained using supervised fine-tuning plus dual-reward reinforcement learning. Experiments show it improves scores by 7 and 24 points respectively on two benchmarks, and all resources are fully open-source.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09382" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Exploring Weight-Space Programming: Capabilities, Generalization, and Limitations</a>

*HF ★ 4 · Junru Hu, Cheng Tang, Wei Li… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.09382)*

This paper focuses on Weight-Space Programming (WSP) technology that can directly edit neural network parameters and replace gradient adaptation, conducting 16 groups of editing trajectory tests based on 7 types of benchmark tasks and two scales of Qwen3.5 models. The results show that WSP can achieve a maximum accuracy improvement of nearly 30 percentage points by modifying only over a thousand parameters, has potential for cross-task generalization but uneven performance, and currently still has bottlenecks such as unstable editing effects and difficult verification.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06043" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Confucius4-R2T2: A Stable-Prefix Streaming ASR Model for Real Real-Time Transcription</a>

*HF ★ 4 · Daniel Huang, Qi He, Shaoxiong Duan… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06043)*

To address the problem that modifying already output tokens in streaming speech recognition easily causes computing power waste and triggers incorrect actions, this paper proposes the concept of Longest Stable Prefix (LSP), freezes the pre-trained audio encoder and projection layer of Qwen3-ASR-1.7B, and obtains the Confucius4-R2T2 model after post-training with 1500 hours of Chinese and English audio. Without modifying the architecture, it can realize that the output prefix is never modified, its accuracy is close to that of full-context models, with low latency and good cross-language generalization, providing a feasible path for converting full-context ASR to stable streaming, and the weights and code have been open-sourced.

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the theoretical context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed the idea that superintelligence can surpass humans and iteratively optimize itself, and in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that RSI is a feedback mechanism for AI to optimize the underlying cognitive architecture relying on its own capabilities. The research points out that the current implementation of AI RSI is divided into two categories: one is directly rewriting its own weights, and the other is optimizing its own training pipeline in a broader sense.
