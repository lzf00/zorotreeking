# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26232" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Time Series Structure Learning with Local Causal Parent Discovery</a>

*HF ★ 2 · Jacqueline Maasch, Shu Wan, Hank Zhu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26232)*

To address pain points in time series causal discovery including observational autocorrelation, distribution drift, curse of dimensionality, and high computational and sample complexity of general algorithms, this study proposes the Iterative Temporal Parent Discovery (ITPD) algorithm. It adopts a local-to-global approach, leveraging reduced-order conditional independence tests with temporal priors, graph partitioning and other techniques. Experiments show it requires fewer tests, achieves Pareto optimality in accuracy and time consumption, and is far faster than baselines in thousand-node scenarios, with a maximum speedup of 40.9x for 2000 nodes.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26239" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely</a>

*HF ★ 9 · Zheyuan Liu, Chuhan Wang, Jinduo Guo… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26239)*

Existing vision-language-action (VLA) robot models cannot safely abort ongoing prohibited tasks, leading to safety hazards such as falling objects. To solve this problem, this paper proposes the State-Aware Fallback Imitation (SAFI) method: it sets fixed robotic arm instructions and stable gripper targets for prohibited tasks, and uses a joint adaptation strategy to retain original task capabilities. Tests show the method reduces the success rate of prohibited tasks after takeover to 1.3%, with no physical accidents, while maintaining high performance on original tasks.

<FeedbackButtons lang="en" slug="item:arxiv-2611.26239" />

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25604" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PAD: Policy-Aware Data Valuation for Autonomous Driving</a>

*HF ★ 4 · Chengkai Xu, Yicheng Guo, Jiaqi Liu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25604)*

Current data filtering methods for end-to-end autonomous driving are not associated with downstream policy utility. To address this pain point, this paper proposes the Policy-Aware Data Valuation (PAD) framework, which combines scenario calibration feedback, policy gradients and local training geometric regularization approximations to calculate exclusive data utility for target driving policies. Experiments show PAD is compatible with multiple types of planners, improves training metrics by 3.5-5 percentage points over baselines, reduces scenario degradation, replaces indiscriminate data accumulation, and improves training efficiency.

<FeedbackButtons lang="en" slug="item:arxiv-2611.25604" />

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15941" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Lang-SVG: Hierarchical Image Vectorization with Language Priors</a>

*HF ★ 1 · Xi Liu, Chaoyi Zhou, Run Wang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.15941)*

Existing image vectorization methods only pursue pixel-level restoration, and the generated SVGs do not conform to human-perceived component hierarchies and are difficult to edit. To solve this problem, this paper proposes Lang-SVG: it organizes SVG primitives in a tree structure with semantic labels, integrates open vocabulary segmentation priors, multimodal LLM annotations, pruning optimization and other technologies, and is paired with a new structural quality evaluation protocol. Experiments show it achieves state-of-the-art performance in both rendering accuracy and structural quality.

<FeedbackButtons lang="en" slug="item:arxiv-2611.15941" />

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25200" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Squoosh Bench: Evaluating UX-1 on 318 Historical Website A/B Tests</a>

*HF ★ 3 · Rohith Pasula, Johari Dramiga, Viraj Shah… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25200)*

To meet the demand for pre-screening revision plans before website A/B testing, the study selected data from 318 historical A/B tests that ran for more than 21 days, masked test background information, and compared multiple prediction systems: the Squoosh UX-1 model fine-tuned on human browsing data predicts test results with 84% accuracy by simulating visitor behavior, far exceeding the 61.6% of the strongest general LLM Qwen3 Next 80B. The revisions it judges as winning have an actual win rate of over 93%.

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the conceptual context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, scholar I.J. Good first proposed the idea of a superintelligent machine, a system that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better machines on its own; In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clearly defined RSI as the feedback loop where AI iterates its own cognitive architecture relying on existing intelligence. Currently, there are two main paths for modern AI to implement RSI: directly rewriting its own weights, or broadly optimizing its own training pipeline.
