## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22115" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks</a>

*HF ★ 94 · Xingchen AGI Lab, Baotian Hu, Bingkai Yang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.22115)*

The newly released Xing 4 is an iterative large model from the TeleChat series, fully compatible with domestic Ascend chips and the MindSpore framework across the entire stack: at the architecture level, it balances performance and computing overhead through sparse activation, attention optimization and other methods; on the training side, it can complete pretraining of a 285-billion parameter model using 8192 Ascend 910C cards; post-training is supported by the TeleGym agent platform, which can efficiently support iterative development of inference and agent tasks.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17621" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AscendGYM: A Cost-Aware Kernel Optimization Benchmark for Real-World Workloads on Ascend NPUs</a>

*HF ★ 66 · AI Training Platform Team, Bingwei Zhang, Jing Li… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.17621)*

To address the gap that existing Ascend NPU kernel benchmarks focus on generation capability and do not cover the evaluation of optimization effects of existing implementations under real workloads, the research team launched the AscendGYM benchmark adapted to real operating environments. It includes 48 kernels taken from real task configurations across 3 difficulty levels, supports two evaluation modes, and also proposes the STS metric that takes both speedup ratio and token cost into account, which can accurately evaluate the actual effectiveness of optimization solutions. It is now open-source.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22055" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Weight-Space Programming Doesn't Need Transformers</a>

*HF ★ 38 · Hyunjong Ok, Jaeho Lee · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.22055)*

This paper targets the gradient-free training weight-space programming scenario, verifying that there is no need to follow the Transformer architecture: guided by only a small number of labeled samples, cutting-edge large models can independently design inference architectures, and the resulting sparse lookup table model has higher accuracy than the optimal Transformer generated by the same large model, with only 1/1800 of its parameters. The associative memory model proposed based on this has a GPU inference speed 17 times faster than Transformer of the same scale, and this advantage only applies to scenarios where weights are written manually.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models</a>

*HF ★ 36 · Ke Sun, Kevin Yuchen Ma, Yike Zhang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21099)*

To address the pain point that frozen multimodal large models only rely on images for zero-shot robot manipulation and cannot utilize contact force, this paper proposes the ForceHarness framework: no fine-tuning or demonstration is required, it implements a force control closed loop via self-terminating force primitives to solve the API response latency problem, and converts force flow into physical quantities with uncertainty that the model can perform inference on through a physical conversion layer. Tests on robotic arms without wrist force sensors show it increases the zero-shot success rate of GPT-6 Astra from 3.3% to 90%, its simulation performance matches that of manually built expert controllers, and outperforms force perception strategies trained via demonstration.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19800" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Multi-Branch Transformer</a>

*HF ★ 24 · Gaotang Li, Lingjie Chen, Chunyuan Deng… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.19800)*

This paper addresses the limitation that traditional Transformer only scales by widening and deepening, proposing Multi-Branch Transformer (MBT) with three branch variants: wide-shallow, narrow-deep, and balanced. Inputs pass through shared initial layers, then run through independent parameter branches in parallel before fusion, paired with Joint-KV shared KV cache to optimize long context window inference. Under the same parameters and training computing power, the optimal solution has 26% higher training efficiency than traditional Transformer, 34% less KV cache, and 51.4% higher decoding throughput on a single H200, providing a new path for large model scaling that balances performance and inference efficiency.

# arXiv cs.LG

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21688" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DeltaHarness: Self-Evolving Harnesses for Resource-Adaptive Tool Execution</a>

*Lecheng Yan, Jianze Lin, Xicheng Han…*

To address the problem that the execution scheduling of tool-calling agents is easily disturbed by runtime uncertainties and latency estimation is inaccurate, this paper proposes the DeltaHarness self-evolving scheduling framework: it supports cold start adaptation, adopts execution-guided policy search, iteratively updates scheduling logic and optimizes execution plans through the QHI loop, and is equipped with an independent verification panel to manage policy updates. Tests show that latency for video editing tasks is reduced by 30%, and latency for unknown tasks is reduced by up to 8.2% compared to the best fixed solution.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21691" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">AdaFlowLite: Scalable and Non-blocking Inference on Asynchronous Mobile Data</a>

*Sicong Liu, Fengmin Wu, Yuan Gao…*

To address the problems that asynchronous arrival of mobile multimodal sensing data easily causes inference latency or accuracy degradation, and existing solutions have insufficient adaptability, AdaFlowLite is proposed: it models cross-modal associations through layered normalization matrices, paired with a lightweight multimodal Swin Transformer to implement retraining-free dynamic data completion, and supports starting inference as soon as partial data arrives. Tests show that compared with existing solutions, latency is reduced by up to 80.4%, accuracy is increased by up to 62.1%, energy consumption is halved, and it can also assist large models in preprocessing asynchronous data.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21694" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">TIBECG: Temporal Information Bottleneck for Lead-Specific Demographic Privacy in ECG Waveform Release</a>

*Tsai-Min Chen (Research Center for Information Technology Innovation, Academia Sinica, Taipei…*

To address the problem that electrocardiogram (ECG) waveform transmission easily leaks demographic privacy, this research proposes the temporal information bottleneck method TIBECG, which can be used for single-lead ECG release scenarios without training on age or gender labels. Verified on 6386 sets of 12-lead data, this method can retain more than 95% of diagnostic utility, the median privacy protection effect is 22.08 percentage points higher than traditional methods, it balances diagnostic performance better than direct downsampling, and the processing effect needs to be evaluated differentially by lead.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) originated from the concept of "ultraintelligent machine" proposed by I.J. Good in 1965, referring to a system that can surpass all human intellectual activities and independently design better machines to achieve self-iteration. In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive architecture relying on its existing intelligence. Currently, RSI in the AI field can narrowly refer to models directly rewriting their own weights, and broadly also covers models optimizing their own training processes.
