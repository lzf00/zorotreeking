## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05320" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">PyRUA-DataFarm: LLM Agent as a Cost-Efficient Teleoperator for Real-Ready Robot Data</a>

*HF ★ 13 · Kewei Zhang, Juncheng Ma, Guodong Lv… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05320)*

This paper proposes the PyRUA-DataFarm robot data generation framework, which uses LLM code agents to replace manual teleoperation for data collection: only 1 calibration sample of the target scene is required to generate usable real-world data with action annotations and multi-view synchronization in simulation. Tests across 5 tasks show that the performance of policies trained on this data is comparable to those trained on manually collected data, its anti-interference robustness far exceeds the latter, and collection costs are significantly lower than manual teleoperation costs in China and the US.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06918" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">From Pixels to Actions - Towards Native Vision-Language-Action Policies</a>

*HF ★ 11 · Weichen Fan, Haiwen Diao, Penghao Wu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06918)*

To address the flaw of existing vision-language-action models that rely on fixed vision encoders and easily lose fine-grained visual information critical for manipulation, this paper proposes the encoder-free architecture NEO-VLA: images are processed via lightweight patch convolution and then jointly computed with language throughout the backbone network, and the action module can call instruction-aligned visual representations of different depths. Experiments show that its performance and robustness are better across various manipulation scenarios and distribution shifts, validating the conclusion that embodied control requires access to cross-level visual representations.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02857" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Solv3R: 3D Looped Transformer as a Multi-view Solver</a>

*HF ★ 8 · Jeong-gi Kwak, Andrea Tagliasacchi, Sho Kagami… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.02857)*

To address the issue that traditional structure from motion supports iterative optimization and incremental expansion, while existing geometric foundation models are difficult to adapt to such requirements with single forward pass or sequential updates, this paper proposes Solv3R, a looped Transformer whose update operator is not limited by the number of iterations and supports caching and reuse of solved views. A single model can cover multiple tasks including offline/incremental reconstruction, relocalization, and stream processing without task-specific training, outperforming current state-of-the-art levels.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01120" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StreamDuo: A Live Commentator and a Research Agent for Unseen Games</a>

*HF ★ 7 · Zixian Gao, Penghao Luo, Xiangcheng Cao… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.01120)*

To address the problem that existing game live commentary LLMs only adapt to games seen during training and suffer from knowledge gaps when generalizing across games, this paper proposes the StreamDuo architecture: it pairs a trainable commentary module with a frozen game wiki retrieval agent, requiring only two-stage training of the commentary module on a single game. After training on *Baldur's Gate 3*, it was tested on 10 unknown games, with entity recall rate significantly improved, source game leakage rate reduced from 9.9% to 1.0%, and commentary timeliness, vividness and other indicators all reaching optimal levels.

# arXiv cs.LG

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05436" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Constraint-Preserving Flow Matching for Spectral Graph Generation</a>

*Anita Yang, Ken-ichi Kawarabayashi, Yohan Jung*

To address the problem that existing spectral graph generation methods only preserve the orthogonality of Laplacian eigenvectors and do not constrain the null space, resulting in generated eigen systems that do not conform to the graph structure, this paper proposes LaSFM, a Laplacian-constrained Stiefel flow matching method. It constructs flow matching on the corresponding constrained manifold, simultaneously ensuring null space conditions, eigenvector orthogonality and eigenvalue positivity, and also supports attribute-guided generation. Experiments show that it achieves significant improvements in spectral, graph-level metrics and attribute alignment effects.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05439" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learning Implicit Solves for Dynamic Simulations</a>

*Chawit Kaewnuratchadasorn, Jiaji Wang, Xin Nie*

To address the pain point of high computing cost for high-fidelity dynamic simulation, this study proposes INOS, an implicit neural operator solver: it pre-trains neural operators based on static equilibrium solutions, and embeds them into the Newmark-β integration framework to solve dynamic problems without dynamic trajectory supervision. Experiments show that its zero-shot accuracy outperforms similar baselines, non-linear simulation is 4600 times faster than traditional finite element methods, it supports cross-spatiotemporal discrete reuse, and can stably complete million-step simulation runs, making it an efficient and robust dynamic modeling paradigm.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05442" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Matched but Not Better: An Equivalence-Tested Benchmark of Quantum Machine Learning on Six Clinical Datasets and the QML-Health-READY Reporting Framework</a>

*Mohammed Siraj B, Gousia Habib, Ruban S*

This study compares quantum kernel support vector machines, variational quantum classifiers and 5 tuned classical models, conducts rigorous equivalence testing and ablation analysis based on 6 public clinical datasets, and proposes QML-Health-READY, an 8-dimensional reporting specification for quantum machine learning in medical scenarios. The results show that the two types of quantum models have comparable performance to classical models but no advantages, limited sampling is the main application bottleneck, configuration errors can easily mislead conclusions, and the new framework can ensure the credibility of results.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/statebench" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">StateBench: Testing how language models track state changes over long documents</a>

*Hugging Face*

To address the issue that existing long text benchmarks mostly focus on static information recall and lack targeted evaluation of LLMs' ability to track dynamic states in long documents, this study constructs the StateBench test set, covering multiple types of cross-paragraph dynamic change scenarios such as entity attributes and association relationships. Actual tests show that even if the context window of current mainstream LLMs meets the length requirement, their long document state update accuracy is still far lower than that of short texts, with core errors being state conflicts and historical information confusion.

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

The concept of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) was first proposed by I.J. Good in 1965, with the core that superintelligent systems that can surpass humans can independently design better models to achieve iterative upgrades; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified its core logic as AI relying on existing intelligence to optimize its own cognitive architecture to form a feedback loop. Currently, this mechanism can be implemented through two paths: directly rewriting its own weights and optimizing training pipelines.
