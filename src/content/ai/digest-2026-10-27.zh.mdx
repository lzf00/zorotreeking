## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33135" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">WorldCast: World-Consistent Character Video Generation with Large-Angle Camera Control</a>

*HF ★ 37 · Lei Cao, Yu Liu, Wenhua Liao… · [HF 镜像](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33135)*

*HF ★ 23 · Zhiyong Cao, Hengjie Zhu, Yongxin Wang… · [HF 镜像](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32285)*

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31716" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Does LeWorldModel Preserve Local Action Geometry?</a>

*Vasil Vasilev, Siddharth Nayak*

本研究验证预训练LeWorldModel（LeWM）对局部动作几何的保留能力，通过匹配模拟器与模型响应，在两类控制任务、三类扰动尺度下用格拉姆矩阵测算动作敏感性。结果显示小扰动下模型最敏感动作方向与真实环境偏差36°~42°，扰动放大后偏差显著降低，LeWM小尺度误差更高，需分尺度评估动作敏感性，其对控制的影响仍待探索。
