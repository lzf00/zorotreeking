*OpenAI*

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the evolution of the concept of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed that a superintelligent system can surpass all human intellectual activities and can design better machines to achieve self-iteration; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive architecture based on existing intelligence. Current RSI in the AI field can be divided into two categories: directly rewriting its own weights, and optimizing training pipelines.
