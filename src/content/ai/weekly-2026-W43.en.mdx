There are far fewer flashy conceptual studies than a while ago, all are practical, directly reusable actionable content. On the investment research side, the density of supportive policies for consumption and hard technology has picked up significantly, coupled with the concentrated release of Q3 earnings reports, the signal of capital position adjustment is very clear.

Personally, I think the most worth revisiting content is the inference framework related materials in the mid-week AI digest issues, as well as the detailed northbound holding data in the stock daily reports from the same period. I added extra annotations when organizing these contents, so their information density is much higher than ordinary news flashes.
