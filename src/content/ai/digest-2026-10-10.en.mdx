# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper conducts research on enabling engineering for AI Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI). The concept of RSI was first proposed by Irving John Good in 1965, referring to superintelligent machines that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better systems on their own to complete iterations; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. Research shows that the paths for contemporary AI to achieve RSI include both directly rewriting its own weights and covering training process optimization.
