## <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Previewing the Model Hardware Standard</a>

*Anthropic*

AI lab Anthropic recently released a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a unified shared specification for AI agents, with the core purpose of providing standardized operating guidelines for AI to safely control various physical devices. Currently, this preview version is only available for trial use by the first batch of selected research laboratories and advanced manufacturing enterprises.

## <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">How Claude&#x27;s text watermarking works</a>

*Anthropic*

Anthropic announced that all text generated by its Claude series of LLMs will have built-in watermark identifiers in the future, which can be used for traceability to determine whether text is generated by Claude. This measure is to comply with the requirements of the EU AI Act, and will be implemented in sync with other mainstream AI vendors. The official also publicly responded to core public concerns including the technical principle of the watermark, its impact on output quality, and the motivation for implementation.

# Google DeepMind

## <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-omni-1-1-flash-lets-you-build-with-more-control/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash lets you build with more control</a>

*Google DeepMind*

Google's newly released Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash is a lightweight multimodal LLM for developers, with its core advantage being significantly improved development controllability: it supports mandatory structured output and fine-grained configuration of multimodal scheduling, has 30% faster inference speed and 40% lower calling cost compared to the previous generation, and can efficiently adapt to customized development needs for scenarios such as agents and multimodal applications.

## <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/piloting-the-worlds-first-double-blind-ai-evaluations/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Piloting the world's first double-blind AI evaluations</a>

*Google DeepMind*

This is the world's first double-blind AI evaluation pilot project, which adopts a design where the identities of the evaluator and the AI subject to be evaluated are hidden from each other. It can effectively avoid non-capability-related interference factors such as brand effect and evaluators' subjective preferences, greatly improving the fairness and credibility of AI capability evaluation results, and providing a more scientific new evaluation paradigm for subsequent scenarios such as AI performance ranking, compliance verification, and market-oriented model selection.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the evolution of the concept of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good first proposed the idea of superintelligent machines, referring to agents that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better systems on their own to achieve self-upgrade; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is that AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on its existing intelligence. Currently, RSI in the AI field includes two paths: models directly rewrite their own weights, and optimize training pipelines to complete iterations.
