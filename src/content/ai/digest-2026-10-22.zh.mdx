*HF ★ 13 · Ruopeng Gao, Yukun Huang, Jiarui Hu… · [HF 镜像](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29989)*

*HF ★ 12 · Xinxin Liu, Bingxin Xu, Wenrui Bao… · [HF 镜像](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28589)*
