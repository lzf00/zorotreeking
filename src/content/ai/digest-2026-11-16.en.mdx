# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18359" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Envisioning Agentic Robotics: A New Era of Robotic Systems</a>

*HF ★ 28 · Grid Robotics, Qingfeng Lan, Duo Li… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18359)*

This paper points out that GPT-6 Astra has spawned a new paradigm for agentic robotics, eliminating the need to train separate strategies for each type of task, and can realize closed-loop decision-making for open-world embodied tasks relying on the base model. Addressing the current lack of unified definitions, evaluation systems and research roadmaps in the field, the authors propose the "Embodied System 2" composed of a base model plus embodied toolkit as the decision-making core, paired with a two-dimensional evaluation framework, clarifying the core challenges and incremental research roadmap for physical autonomous deployment.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18397" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gamer: A Generalist Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Long-Horizon Real-Time Game Agent</a>

*HF ★ 18 · Run Luo, Kuan Zhang, Qi Deng… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18397)*

This paper proposes Gamer, a generalist vision-language-action foundation model for long-horizon real-time game tasks. It adopts an end-to-end architecture integrating perception, memory and inference modules, is pretrained on tens of thousands of hours of keyboard and mouse interaction trajectories, optimized via sparse chain-of-thought instruction tuning and multi-round reinforcement learning training strategies, and paired with a cloud-edge inference engine to control latency. The model performs on par with senior players on 2D and 3D game benchmarks, providing new ideas for the research and development of general agents across digital and physical worlds.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07226" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Loopback Transformer: Towards a Unified View of Residual Looped Transformers</a>

*HF ★ 10 · Xuan Ouyang, Zefan Cai, Chenlong Zhang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.07226)*

Aiming at the problem that recurrent Transformers only rely on parameter sharing and cannot clarify calculation result reuse rules, this paper optimizes from the perspective of deep storage of residual connections, and proposes Loopback Transformer: it separately retains the input path, sets total update weights with geometric decay, and dynamically allocates weights via attention. At the same training cost, it achieves lower validation loss when its parameter count is only 40%-75% of the baseline, and its average accuracy on 13 downstream tasks outperforms standard Transformer and 7 similar recurrent architectures.

<FeedbackButtons slug="item:arxiv-2611.07226" client:visible />

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14504" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Emergent Biological Capabilities in a Foundation Model for Molecular Interactions</a>

*HF ★ 8 · Adi Mashiach · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.14504)*

The research team developed Bonbon, a foundation model for molecular interactions. Trained only on 3.2 trillion pairs of protein-ligand sequences, without requiring structural data or task-specific annotations, it emerged with three types of cross-level zero-shot molecular interaction recognition capabilities with excellent generalization performance. The capabilities emerge abruptly as parameter count increases, which cannot be predicted by pretraining loss. Wet lab verification shows the target inhibition hit rate of new compounds reaches 61.5%, with leading performance and inference speed far exceeding traditional co-folding methods.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18338" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MEM-SQL: Memory-Empowered Text-to-SQL</a>

*HF ★ 6 · Xiaoqi Wang, Wenqi Pei, Shizheng Hou… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18338)*

Addressing the problem that existing Text-to-SQL benchmarks ignore the reusability of historical queries from the same database, researchers built the HARE benchmark containing 1600 requests to evaluate the accuracy and overhead of historical SQL reuse. They proposed the MEM-SQL method, which uses hierarchical storage of reusable SQL resources paired with on-demand Schema linking. On HARE, its accuracy is 12.9 percentage points higher than direct generation without historical data, input tokens are reduced by 76.6%, latency is reduced by 68.8%, and the on-demand linking mechanism can generally reduce the generation cost of similar memory architectures.

# arXiv cs.LG

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17398" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alignment with Reflect-Reasoning: Learning How To Follow Principles</a>

*Henry Bell, Caroline Zhang, Dhaval Potdar…*

Aiming at the problem that existing LLM alignment technologies mostly rely on fixed preset rules, and reflection reasoning methods are limited by the native capabilities of the model, this paper proposes a reflection reasoning training method that teaches the model to master the general reasoning logic of alignment rules rather than just memorizing fixed rules. This method outperforms baseline strategies on 5 types of models, can be generalized to out-of-domain scenarios such as healthcare and finance, can reduce the risk of serious violations and resist jailbreak attacks, and can adapt to new rules without additional fine-tuning.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17412" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">WETW: Learning Pair-Specific Weighted Euclidean Distances for Efficient DTW Search</a>

*Christos Panourgias, Qitong Wang, George Tzagkarakis…*

Aiming at the low efficiency of dynamic time warping (DTW) time series similarity search, and the problem that existing learning-based metrics use fixed rules that do not match the pairwise alignment characteristics of DTW, this paper proposes the WETW adaptive metric, which uses a symmetric network to predict pairwise embedding weights, paired with a WESEE encoder that preserves time series information and ranking regularization, along with a filter-verify pipeline WETW-FV. Actual measurements show it is 11.4 times faster than the current fastest accurate DTW search method, with speedup up to 40 times for long time series, and recall rate far outperforms baselines at the same speed.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17459" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Classification with Large-Step Gradient Descent: Unifying Self-Stabilization and Convergence Rates</a>

*Salma Tarmoun, Lachlan Ewen MacDonald, Konstantinos Emmanouilidis…*

Aiming at the problem that existing theories of large-step gradient descent for training classifiers are fragmented and not suitable for classification scenarios, this paper extends the unified geometric perspective to exponential tail losses such as logistic and cross-entropy, replaces the minimization manifold with a reference subspace defined by curvature, derives the iteration boundary across stable/non-stable regions, reveals the three-stage damping structure, and also finds that the transient implicit bias of large step sizes affects the alignment effect between finite time and maximum margin direction.

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the research context of recursive self-improvement (RSI): I.J. Good first proposed the relevant concept in 1965, believing that superintelligent machines can surpass all human intellectual activities and achieve self-iteration by designing better systems; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence.
