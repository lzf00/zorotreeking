# Hugging Face Daily Papers

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30932" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UserArena: Benchmarking Interactive User Simulation for Long-Horizon Shopping Agents</a>

*HF ★ 19 · Jujia Zhao, Jiakai Tang, Jing Yu… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30932)*

To address the pain point that long-horizon intelligent recommendation agents for e-commerce lack real interaction evaluation benchmarks and are hard to align with online A/B test results, this paper proposes the UserArena interaction benchmark, which covers 64,700 real conversation trajectories from 11,000 users, includes over 560,000 products and 13 types of interaction actions, and supports 5-level full-link offline evaluation. The paper also proposes the on-policy trajectory distillation method OPTD, which improves the fidelity of long-interaction simulation relying on trajectory-level intent. Experiments show it performs best on this benchmark and is closest to the actual effect of online policies.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32253" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RSIHub: A Composable Framework for Agent-Evolution Methods</a>

*HF ★ 17 · Zimu Wang, Xiaobo Wang, Siyu Ye… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32253)*

To address the pain point of repeated development of experimental bases for agent evolution methods, this paper proposes the composable open-source framework RSIHub: it adopts a recipe mechanism of reusable operators + variable resource declarations, with built-in general capabilities including execution, evaluation, and traceability. Researchers can adapt to various evolution strategies without modifying the core code. Evaluations show that evolution methods built on this framework improve pass@1 by up to 13.5 percentage points, but method rankings vary by scenario, and optimization benefits cannot always be transferred across tasks.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32965" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RynnUnify: Towards Unified Embodied Brain</a>

*HF ★ 8 · Minghao Zhu, Bohan Hou, Kun Xiang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32965)*

This paper proposes RynnUnify, a 16B-parameter unified embodied model. Relying on interleaved video-language reasoning, it has both embodied understanding and long-sequence visual planning capabilities, paired with extended RL agent capabilities, world action extensions adapted for robot control, and an exclusive evaluation benchmark. Experiments show its performance on multiple embodied tasks is significantly better than existing baselines, and it can serve as a general foundation for embodied intelligence, taking a step toward the goal of a unified embodied brain.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27103" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Omni-Woodpecker: Hallucination Correction via Evidence-Prior Rebalancing for Omni LLMs</a>

*HF ★ 7 · Zijian Liu, Chaoyou Fu, Yan Yang… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.27103)*

To address the cross-modal hallucination problem in audio-visual reasoning of omni-modal LLMs, existing solutions that only intervene at the output layer fail to solve the entanglement defect between factual evidence and training priors in fused representations. This paper proposes the training-free framework Omni-Woodpecker: it first identifies cross-modal conflicts, orthogonally splits representations, and rebalances the weights of evidence and priors before decoding to suppress hallucinations. This framework has strong cross-architecture generalization, delivers stable performance improvements on multiple mainstream models and benchmarks, and its code is open-sourced.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31569" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Echo: Coupled Action and Task Status Signal Denoising for Vision Language Action Models</a>

*HF ★ 2 · Yufeng Liu, Bohao Fan, Juntu Zhao… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31569)*

Existing Vision-Language Action (VLA) models require fine-grained annotation and additional supporting modules to add task state prediction, and do not fully exploit the guiding value of state signals for action generation. To address this pain point, this paper proposes the Echo framework: it does not require frame-level progress annotation, jointly denoises two types of state signals (stop and failure) as action dimensions, constructs three types of trajectories to implement weak supervision, and allows state signals to be denoised faster to guide action generation. Real-world experiments show its state prediction is accurate, and strategy performance is stably improved.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This article sorts out the conceptual context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): in 1965, scholar Irving John Good first proposed the concept of "ultraintelligent machines" that can surpass all human intellectual activities and independently design better machines; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that the core of RSI is the feedback loop where AI iteratively optimizes its own cognitive architecture relying on its existing intelligence. RSI in current AI scenarios includes both models directly rewriting their own weights, and can also broadly refer to models optimizing their own training processes.
