## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27851" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LaLa-World: Scaling Latent World Models with Natural Language Actions</a>

*HF ★ 6 · Junha Song, Geonmo Gu, Jeongeun Park… · [HF Mirror](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.27851)*

This paper proposes LaLa-World, a language-conditioned latent world model, which achieves cross-domain training through two core designs: converting numerical actions and visual changes into unified language representations, and building a predictor based on large language models that can integrate multi-source data including simulation, robot trajectories, image-text and video. Its planning performance outperforms similar single-domain models, reducing training cost by 5/6 and increasing planning speed by 13.9x compared to the RAEv2 baseline; the derived LaLa-Policy matches the performance of state-of-the-art models on robot tasks, with inference speed up to 5.5x faster.

# arXiv cs.LG

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28637" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">To Keep or Not to Keep: Learning KV Cache Retention in Disaggregated LLM Serving Systems</a>

*Dong Liu, Yanxuan Yu, Eric Jiang…*

To address the problem that traditional KV cache in disaggregated LLM deployment does not consider cross-node transmission overhead and easily wastes memory and bandwidth, this paper proposes KVLearn, a learning-based KV cache retention framework that does not require modifying model weights, and can make dynamic decisions combining prefix reuse probability, multi-dimensional costs and real-time load. Tests show its first token latency is reduced by up to 56%, cross-node transmission volume is reduced by up to 53%, and multimodal throughput is close to the ideal value.

## <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28693" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Interaction-Gated Time-Frequency Learning for Parameter-Efficient PPG-Based Heart Rate Estimation</a>

*Anh Khoi Pham, Phat Lam, Thien Truong Nguyen Ly…*

To solve the pain points that photoplethysmography (PPG) heart rate estimation is susceptible to interference, and lightweight models struggle to efficiently integrate complementary time-frequency information, this paper proposes the parameter-efficient TF-IG framework: dual branches extract time-domain pulse morphology features and frequency-domain heartbeat cycle features respectively, which are adaptively fused through an interaction gating module, and output results via a shared regression head. On the BIDMC benchmark dataset, it achieves a MAE as low as 1.227 BPM with only 84.6K parameters, realizing a good balance between accuracy and lightweight design.

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the conceptual evolution of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, I.J. Good proposed the idea of ultra-intelligent machines, referring to systems whose capabilities surpass all human intellectual activities and can design better machines to complete self-upgrades; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky defined RSI as a feedback loop where AI iterates its own cognitive architecture relying on existing intelligence. In the current AI context, RSI can refer to the model directly rewriting its own weights, or broadly cover the behavior of the model optimizing its own training process.
