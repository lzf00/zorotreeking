## <a href="https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Combatting adversarial distillation</a>

*OpenAI*

This study addresses the protection demand of large models against adversarial distillation, an attack method that maliciously extracts core model capabilities. It introduces a layered defense framework launched by OpenAI, which integrates four modules: model training optimization, security protection mechanism, abnormal attack detection, and violation handling and enforcement. The framework can systematically resist the risk of capability theft, providing an implementable technical path for the protection of core intellectual property of large models.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/harness-adapters-at-scale" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Introducing harness adapters: adapt your favorite model to your harness to improve performance and cost</a>

*Hugging Face*

This paper proposes a lightweight component "harness adapter", which serves as a universal intermediate adaptation layer between pretrained models and downstream training frameworks (i.e., harnesses). It can quickly complete the adaptation between the two without extensive modification of the model structure or the original code of the training pipeline. This solution can stably improve the performance of downstream tasks, while significantly reducing adaptation development costs and training computing power overhead, and is compatible with mainstream open-source models and commonly used training frameworks.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the research context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): the concept was first proposed by I.J. Good in 1965, referring to "ultraintelligent machines" that can surpass all human intellectual activities and design better systems on their own to achieve iterative upgrades; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified its core logic as AI relying on its existing intelligence to optimize its own cognitive base. Currently, the RSI implementation paths for modern AI are mainly divided into two categories: one is the model directly rewriting its own weights, and the other is optimizing its own training pipeline in a broad sense.

# QbitAI

## <a href="https://www.qbitai.com/2026/11/511779.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GPT-6.2 Astra outperforms embodied models again: success rate on 16 tasks doubles compared to GPT-6 Astra</a>

*QbitAI*

XDOF adopted the Ember-0 embodied evaluation benchmark for dual-arm robots oriented to real physical environments. Through a rigorous process of parallel testing on 6 workstations, repeating each task 30 times and filtering out hardware deviations, it was measured that the average success rate of GPT-6.2 Astra controlling the robot to complete 16 operations reached 41.2%, doubling that of the previous generation. It also completed tasks that no AI could do before, such as tying knots and unfolding T-shirts, for the first time, and existing benchmarks will soon be saturated by its capabilities.
