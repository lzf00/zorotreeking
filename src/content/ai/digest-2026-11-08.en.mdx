# OpenAI

## <a href="https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-hacktron" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Partnering with Hacktron to find vulnerabilities in OpenAI’s systems</a>

*OpenAI*

OpenAI recently announced a special security partnership with Hacktron, implementing three core measures to strengthen its system security: first, fully investigate unknown security vulnerabilities in OpenAI's full-stack systems; second, test the risk of connection links with various third-party services; third, upgrade the overall security defense system with cutting-edge AI technology, to consolidate the security protection capabilities of its own systems and connected ecosystem from multiple dimensions.

## <a href="https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Measuring how AI agents handle everyday wet-lab science</a>

*OpenAI*

OpenAI has launched BioBasicsBench, a dedicated evaluation benchmark for the biological field, with a total of 100 segmented tasks. It is mainly used to quantitatively evaluate the ability of AI agents to interpret raw wet lab data, as well as their comprehensive performance in handling various scientific research scenarios in daily wet labs. This benchmark fills the gap in AI capability evaluation for practical life science experimental scenarios, and can provide standardized evaluation basis for the iteration of AI agents for biological R&D.

# Hugging Face Blog

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/toyblocks" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ToyBlocks: A controlled world for robot learning and evaluation</a>

*Hugging Face*

Aiming at the pain points of existing robot learning simulation environments such as difficult variable control and poor evaluation reproducibility, this paper proposes ToyBlocks, a modular controllable simulation test environment. It supports custom block splicing tasks, environmental interference parameters and evaluation dimensions, can accurately locate the capability shortcomings of each link of robot perception, manipulation and decision-making, effectively reduce training and debugging costs, and provide standardized verification and evaluation tools for multi-task robot learning research.

## <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/open-asr-leaderboard-liva" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Egyptian and Nigerian English join the Open ASR Leaderboard</a>

*Hugging Face*

The Open Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) public leaderboard recently added evaluation items for two African regional English variants: Egyptian English and Nigerian English. Previously, mainstream ASR models generally had low recognition accuracy for such low-resource non-standard English variants. The addition of corresponding evaluation benchmarks can fill the relevant evaluation gap, guide models to optimize their adaptability to diverse accents, and improve the global inclusiveness of ASR technology.

# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

This paper sorts out the conceptual context of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI): In 1965, Irving John Good first proposed that superintelligent machines can surpass all human intellectual activities and iteratively design better systems; in 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI optimizes its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. In the current AI context, RSI has two implementation paths: one is to directly rewrite its own weights, and the other is to optimize its own training pipeline.

## <a href="https://www.qbitai.com/2026/11/508466.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Capital is withdrawing from "world models"</a>

*QbitAI*

The world model track has cooled rapidly from the venture capital hotspot in the first half of this year, capital investment thresholds have risen sharply, hot money has plummeted, only a handful of projects are favored by top funds, and the track's valuation premium has shrunk rapidly. The window for financing relying solely on concepts continues to narrow, only teams with technical accumulation and actual implementation progress are still sought after, and the market's patience for this track is constantly shrinking.
