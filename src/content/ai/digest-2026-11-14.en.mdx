# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) was first proposed by scholar Irving John Good in 1965, and is the core feature of "superintelligent machines" that can surpass all human intellectual activities and independently design better systems to complete self-iteration. In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is the feedback loop where AI iterates its own cognitive mechanism relying on existing intelligence. In the current AI field, this mechanism includes both models directly rewriting their own weights, and can broadly refer to optimizing their own training processes.
