# Lil'Log

## <a href="https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2026-07-04-harness/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement</a>

*Lilian Weng*

The concept of Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) originated from I.J. Good's 1965 "ultraintelligent machine" hypothesis: such systems can surpass humans in all intellectual activities, and can also design better machines to complete self-iteration. In 2008, Eliezer Yudkowsky clarified that its core is a feedback loop: AI uses its existing intelligence to optimize and generate the cognitive mechanism of its own intelligence. Current RSI in the AI field includes both models directly rewriting their own weights, and can also broadly refer to the behavior of models optimizing their own training pipelines.
